Building a Responsive Dashboard

A worked example: stat cards, a chart panel and a sidebar that collapse cleanly from desktop to phone.

DdevadetayoAtomattr

This is a worked example rather than a customer story: we will build the skeleton of a dashboard and see how a few responsive attributes carry it from a phone to a wide monitor. All numbers on this page are placeholders.

The plan

  • A row of stat cards: one column on phones, two on small tablets, four on desktop.
  • A main panel and a side panel that stack on small screens and sit side by side from the lg breakpoint (1024px).
  • Dark mode handled with the dark- prefix.

Breakpoints are prefixes on any attribute, and they are mobile-first: the plain attribute applies everywhere, and a prefixed one applies from that width upward. The available widths are sm 640px, md 768px, lg 1024px, xl 1280px and 2xl 1536px.

Stat cards

html
<section display="grid" grid-cols="1" sm-grid-cols="2" lg-grid-cols="4" gap="4">
  <div p="5" rounded="2xl" bg="surface" border="1px solid border">
    <p text="muted" font-size="sm" m="0">Visitors</p>
    <p font-size="3xl" font-weight="800" m="0">12,480</p>
  </div>
  <!-- repeat for the other cards -->
</section>

Main panel and side panel

html
<div display="grid" grid-cols="1" lg-grid-cols="3" gap="4" mt="4">
  <div lg-col-span="2" p="6" rounded="2xl" bg="surface" border="1px solid border">Chart</div>
  <aside p="6" rounded="2xl" bg="surface" border="1px solid border">Activity</aside>
</div>

On a phone the two panels stack in source order. From 1024px, the grid becomes three columns and the chart spans two of them. No media query was written by hand.

Dark mode

Give each surface a dark value. Using semantic colors such as surface and border covers most of it; add dark-bg where you want a specific shade. See the dark mode guide.

Things to watch

  • Tables and charts need overflow-x="auto" on their wrapper so they scroll instead of breaking the layout on narrow screens.
  • Prefer min-w="0" on grid children that hold wide content.
  • Test at 360px, 768px and 1280px: the places where layouts usually break.
