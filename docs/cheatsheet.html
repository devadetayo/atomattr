Practice patterns

The fastest way to learn this page is to copy one example, paste it into the playground, and change one attribute at a time. Keep the structure stable while you experiment with values. Once the result feels right, move the same attributes into your real component.

When a component starts to feel noisy, group the attributes mentally by purpose: layout, spacing, color, type, border, effects, and state. Atomattr does not require a special order, but a consistent order makes examples easier to teach and production markup easier to review.
